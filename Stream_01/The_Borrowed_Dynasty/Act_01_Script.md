# The Borrowed Life: My Rich Fiancé Was Rented by a Family Curse

## Metadata Description:
>A proud Lagos lawyer thinks she has found the perfect rich husband — until his grandmother’s blessing reveals something cold, dead, and borrowed beneath his flawless skin.

### VISUAL TONE:
* **Atmosphere/Lighting:** Glossy Lagos luxury slowly curdling into ancestral dread: neon Island nights, champagne gold, beach torchlight, then the heavy brown darkness of an old Ikeja mansion where the air feels cold and cursed.
* **Foreground Action:** Teni’s proud face cracking beat by beat; Tokunbo’s perfect beauty hiding panic; Chief Mrs. Alaba watching like a predator in lace; grandmother’s terrified hands exposing the first impossible truth.
