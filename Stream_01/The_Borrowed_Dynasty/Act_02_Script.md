# The Borrowed Life: My Father Signed Me Into My Fiancé’s Family Curse

## Metadata Description:
>Teni’s perfect engagement begins to rot as Tokunbo’s luxury life disappears car by car, his skin turns cold and gray, and every Adelaja asset traces back to a terrifying trust. When Teni breaks into the forbidden middle room of the Ikeja mansion, she discovers the curse paperwork — and her own father’s signature on the deal that sold her future.

### VISUAL TONE:
* **Atmosphere/Lighting:** Glossy Lagos wedding luxury rotting into legal horror: bright Ikoyi glass, neon dinners, champagne gold, then colder Mainland shadows, damp concrete, flickering bulbs, and ancestral darkness pressing against expensive fabric.
* **Foreground Action:** Teni’s pride curdling into terror; Tokunbo’s beauty cracking into ash-gray decay; silent men repossessing borrowed wealth; her father hiding panic behind respectability; Sade’s trembling warning; cursed contracts exposed in the windowless heart of the Ikeja mansion.

**Narration**: "The Mercedes was still shining in my father’s compound when my grandmother spat into her palm and told me not to enter that marriage alive. Everyone else was outside touching leather seats, laughing too loudly, already drunk on the smell of new money. [VISUAL] But Mama Agba held my wrist with fingers like dry sticks and whispered, 'That boy is not warm because he is not fully here.' I pulled away.
