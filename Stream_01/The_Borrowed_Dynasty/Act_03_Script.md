# The Borrowed Life: Wedding Night at the Cursed Beach House

## Metadata Description:
>Teni discovers her father sold her future twenty years ago, then walks willingly into Alaba Adelaja’s isolated Ilashe trap. With no network, no boat, and her wedding night designed as a ritual execution, she does the one thing nobody expects: she treats the family curse like a contract and finds the clause that can destroy the dynasty.

### VISUAL TONE:
* **Atmosphere/Lighting:** A luxury Lagos nightmare collapsing into coastal ritual horror: rain-slick Mainland roads, fluorescent family shame, moonlit Ilashe glass walls, black river stillness, violent ocean wind, candle-blue supernatural light, and dawn washing bloodless wealth into ash.
* **Foreground Action:** Teni transforms from terrified bride into furious legal avenger; stolen contracts against her skin, father’s confession breaking the family, Tokunbo decaying with guilt, Alaba’s elegance cracking at the altar, and the ledger becoming the weapon that turns the curse back on its Protector.
