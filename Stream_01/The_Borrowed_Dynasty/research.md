# Story Planning: The Borrowed Life (Stream 01)

## The Core Idea
A proud Lagos woman who refuses to marry any normal guy finally finds a perfect, incredibly rich husband. But after the wedding plans start, she finds out his massive wealth, expensive cars, and handsome face are totally rented from a terrifying family curse—and the deadline to pay it back is their wedding night.

## Story Type & Feeling
Lagos high-society drama and scary thriller. 
Expensive, high-stakes, trapped, and very tense. 
Main Theme: The terrible price of trying to look too perfect; dark family lies hidden behind big money.

## Video Length & Pacing
- Total Video Time: 45 minutes (Around 6500 words total)
- Part 1: The Perfect Lie (Ends around 1600 words at the Family Meeting)
- Part 2: The Perfect Lie Breaks (Big discovery at 3200 words)
- Part 3: The Deadly Trap (The scary finale starts at 5000 words)

## Story Chapters
1. The Picky Bride and the Perfect Rich Guy
2. The Cold Blessing (The First Family Meeting)
3. The Fake Wealth Starts to Vanish (Rented Cars and Gray Hands)
4. The Dark Secret in the Ikeja House (Finding out about the Curse)
5. Trapped at the Beach House (The Final Threat)
6. The Hidden Loophole (How She Fights Back)

## Sights and Sounds
- The Island (The Rich Side): Shiny glass buildings, loud AC units, expensive perfume, champagne glasses clinking.
- The Mainland (The Real Side): Heavy old wooden furniture, loud grandfather clock ticking, dark corners, heavy sweating.
- The Curse Signs: Skin turning a dead ash-grey, hands becoming freezing cold, hiding away from direct sunlight.

## The Characters

### Teniola "Teni" Bankole (The Bride)
Age 29. A sharp, tough corporate lawyer who fixes big company deals in Ikoyi. She is highly ambitious. Her parents struggled on the Mainland before making good money, so she refuses to settle for a broke or lazy man. She dumps every guy who tries to control her.
HER BIG FLAW: She is so obsessed with finding a perfect, flawless man that she misses the obvious signs that her new guy is too good to be true.

### Tokunbo "Toks" Adelaja (The Groom)
Age 31. Insanely handsome, gentle, and super rich. He just came back from London and acts like the perfect gentleman.
HIS REAL MOTIVATION: He is just trying to stay alive. He really loves Teni, but his family is secretly ruined. His good looks, smart brain, and big money are on a strict timer from an old family curse. If he doesn’t marry a good, clean girl like Teni and trap her in his family line, the curse will strip away his looks and money, leaving him a hollow, dying shell.

### Chief Mrs. Alaba Adelaja (The Cruel Mother)
Tokunbo’s elegant but terrifying mother. She runs Lagos high society and commands every room she walks into. She is the one who keeps the family curse running and makes the deals to keep them rich.

## The Settings

### The Rich Side (The Island)
Shiny, rich, and cold. Sky-high penthouses in Banana Island, private VIP rooms in Victoria Island, fast night drives over the bright Lekki-Ikoyi link bridge. 

### The Real Side (The Old Family House)
A massive, heavily guarded, scary concrete mansion built in the 1980s in old Ikeja GRA. Dark wood everywhere, faded old wealth, and a center room with no windows where the air is freezing cold.

### The Trap (The Ilashe Beach House)
A lonely luxury beach house you can only get to by boat. The wild ocean is on one side, and a dead silent river is on the other. No escape.

## Part 1 Outline — THE PERFECT LIE

### The Opening
Teni talks about how messy Lagos dating is—full of rich, arrogant boys—and how she refused to settle. Then she meets Tokunbo. He is totally different: sweet, incredibly rich, and loves her career.

### The Romance
A crazy, fast love story that ends with a massive proposal on a private beach. Teni’s family thinks everything is moving way too fast and gets worried, but Teni ignores them because she wants her dream wedding.

### The First Scare
The traditional family introduction party in Ikeja. When Teni’s old grandmother holds Tokunbo’s hands to bless him, she screams, pulls away, and tells Teni that his skin feels like a cold grave. Tokunbo quickly covers it up by giving Teni’s family an outrageously expensive car as a surprise gift.

## Audience Hooks
- The Middle Twist: Teni finds her own father's signature on a dark pact inside the family safe.
- The Big Comment Fight: Should Teni run away and let her father go totally broke, or should she marry the cursed man and destroy his family from the inside?

## Part 2 Outline — THE PERFECT LIE BREAKS

### Chapter 3: The Fake Wealth Starts to Vanish
- **The Core Event:** The wedding preparations begin, but Tokunbo's rented world starts to rot. The luxury cars they used during the courtship are suddenly repossessed by random, silent men because his "lease time" is running out. 
- **The Physical Decay:** Teni notices Tokunbo's skin turning a dry, ash-grey color under his expensive clothes. His breath smells faintly of damp earth. When she confronts him, he blames the stress of organizing a Lagos wedding.

### Chapter 4: The Dark Secret in the Ikeja House
- **The Discovery:** Teni uses her legal skills to trace the title deeds of the Adelaja properties. She finds out every single asset is owned by a shadowy trust registered on the same day Tokunbo was born.
- **The Middle Twist:** Teni sneaks into the windowless center room of the Ikeja mansion. Inside the heavy family safe, she doesn't just find the curse documents—she finds a dark pact with **her own father’s signature** on it from twenty years ago. Her father’s current business success was bought by trading her away to this family.

---

## Part 3 Outline — THE DEADLY TRAP

### Chapter 5: Trapped at the Beach House
- **The Trap:** Chief Mrs. Alaba forces the couple to move to the isolated Ilashe Beach House three days before the wedding "for privacy." Boats are cut off. There is no network.
- **The Reality:** Tokunbo breaks down and confesses. He reveals the strict deadline: on their wedding night, the curse will completely drain her life force to reset his family’s wealth for another thirty years. If she escapes, his family drops dead, and her father goes completely broke and goes to prison.

### Chapter 6: The Hidden Loophole
- **The Climax:** Teni realizes she is a corporate lawyer for a reason. She treats the ancestral curse like a bad business contract. She finds a loophole in the original pact paperwork her father signed.
- **The Resolution:** She doesn't run away. Instead, she forces a restructuring of the "debt" on the wedding night, binding Chief Mrs. Alaba to pay the ultimate price instead, turning the tables on the high-society matriarch and taking control of the entire dynasty.
