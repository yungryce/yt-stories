# The Kill-Slop Checklist

Run these three checks by hand before you lock any final script.

### 1. The Read-Aloud Test
* Action: Read the script out loud. If a sentence sounds flat, robotic, or like a Wikipedia summary, delete it.
* Fix: Cut the textbook voice. Replace it with blood and nerves.
  - BANNED: "The protagonist was feeling anxious about the upcoming meeting with her in-laws."
  - MANDATORY: "Her fingers trembled against the glass, the condensation dripping like the seconds before a verdict. She couldn't let them see her shake."

### 2. The Gut Check
* Action: Scan every paragraph for emotional stakes. If a beat doesn't raise the temperature—fear, dread, hope, rage—it's filler.
* Fix: Turn boring description into something that twists the knife. Every detail should darken the mood or shift who's in control.
  - BANNED: "The family compound was built in the 1980s with mahogany wood and a large parlor."
  - MANDATORY: "The old compound swallowed the light. Mahogany panels pressed in like a coffin lining, and the parlor at its center had no windows—because whatever they did in there was never meant to see the sun."

### 3. The Cliffhanger Test
* Action: Read the last 15 seconds of Act 1 and Act 2.
* Fix: End every act on a question the viewer has to know the answer to. A whispered threat. An unanswered text. A door that should have stayed locked. Make them desperate for Act 2.
